Historia geographic atlas

Renderer: MapLibre GL JS 5.19.0, BSD-3-Clause. Copyright MapLibre contributors.
https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js/tree/v5.19.0
Full license: /vendor/maplibre-LICENSE.txt

Polygon union: polygon-clipping 0.15.7, MIT. Copyright Mike Fogel and contributors.
https://github.com/mfogel/polygon-clipping
Full license: /vendor/polygon-clipping-LICENSE.md

Relief: Natural Earth II, 1:50m, land cover/shaded relief/water.
Natural Earth raster and vector data are public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-raster/tree/f04832c819e7a203d159dffe93cd9245332d6b33/50m_rasters/NE2_50M_LC_SR_W
Coast display refinement: Natural Earth 1:10m land, clipped around historical inland boundaries.
Derived coast-regions.geojson.gz retains the Historical Basemaps GPL-3.0-only data license.
Waterways/lakes: Natural Earth 1:50m, via nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/geojson.
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
Source input checksums and projection: /atlas/build.json and /atlas/coast-build.json

Historical political geography: Historical Basemaps, GPL-3.0-only.
See data-maps/README.md and data-maps/LICENSE in the source repository.
Political sectors and smaller-state groupings are gameplay abstractions.
Modern natural features are geographic context; cities use interwar names.

Open Historia was inspected as a reference for the layered atlas architecture.
https://github.com/Open-Historia/open-historia (AGPL-3.0-or-later)
No Open Historia source code, assets or dependencies were copied into this implementation.
The user-provided Pax Historia HTML was inspected as a visual reference only.
Its scripts, account/session data, and proprietary assets are not shipped or executed.
